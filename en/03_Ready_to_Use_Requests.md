# Ready-to-Use Requests

Upload `01_Upload_to_GPT_Songwriting_Assistant.md`, then choose a request below. Replace the theme, style or supplied text with your own.

## 01 — Start from a simple idea

```text
Use Songwriting Assistant to write an English song about leaving an old city and starting again.
Make it warm, concrete and hopeful, with an easy-to-remember chorus.
Suggest the genre, tempo, voice and form.
Give complete lyrics, Suno Styles, Exclude and an arrangement table.
```

## 02 — Use a specific style

```text
Write an English city-pop song with neo-soul chord color.
Theme: driving home at night, moving from disappointment toward ease.
Warm female vocal, Rhodes and melodic bass, roughly three minutes.
Give complete lyrics, compact/extended Styles, Exclude, form and arrangement.
```

## 03 — Lyrics only

```text
Write English lyrics only; no Styles or arrangement yet.
Theme: returning home years later and realizing I am the one who changed.
Use concrete everyday scenes in the verses and a short memorable chorus.
Avoid abstract filler and forced rhymes. Give a title and complete sectioned lyrics.
```

## 04 — Keep every lyric unchanged

```text
Do not change a word of the lyrics below. Only design the music.
Suggest a primary genre, tempo, structure, harmony and instrument roles.
Give two English Styles versions and Exclude.
Mention any singability concerns outside the lyric block; do not rewrite the text.

My lyrics:
(paste here)
```

## 05 — Rewrite only one section

```text
Rewrite only the chorus below. Keep its final line exactly unchanged.
Keep four lines, use natural conversational English and favor an ABAB rhyme pattern.
Do not change other sections or add a full production report.

Original chorus:
(paste here)
```

## 06 — An instrumental cue

```text
Create an approximately 90-second instrumental for a winter short film.
Piano and cello, moving from restraint to warmth and back to quiet.
A–B–A-prime structure, no drums or vocals.
Give two Styles versions, Instrumental settings, Exclude, form and arrangement.
```

## 07 — Compare genres first

```text
Compare three song styles for the theme “walking home alone at night.”
Use a table for mood, groove, voice, instruments and suitable structure.
Give one short English Styles example for each; do not write full lyrics yet.
```

## 08 — Improve an existing result

```text
Below are my original Styles and lyrics.
Problem: the chorus does not open up and the backing masks the lead vocal.
Address one problem first. Keep the lyrics, primary genre and tempo.
Give revised Styles and one A/B listening comparison.

Original Styles:
(paste here)

Original lyrics:
(paste here)
```

## 09 — Plan the form

```text
Arrange my song into intro, verse one, pre-chorus, chorus, verse two,
bridge, final chorus and outro.
For each section, say what the lyric does, which instruments enter or leave,
and how the emotion changes. Keep the verses spacious and broaden the chorus
register rather than merely making it louder. Do not write new lyrics yet.
```
