# Your Creative Brief

Start with five items. Write “please suggest” for anything you do not know.

## Simple version

Copy this block, fill it in and send it after uploading the assistant file.

```text
Use the uploaded Songwriting Assistant.

Task: complete song / lyrics only / music for existing lyrics / rewrite / instrumental
Theme or scene:
How it should feel and sound:
Lyric language and voice, or instrumental:
Elements to avoid:

Please suggest any other parameters. Give me usable results for this task.
```

You can describe a sound without knowing its genre: “warm and retro, light drums, an intimate vocal” is enough to start.

## Detailed version — add only what matters

### A — Theme and lyrics

```text
Theme and story:
Narrator / who they are addressing:
Lyric language:
Lines or keywords that must remain:
Rhyme, line count or wording requirements:
```

### B — Style and voice

```text
Primary genre:
Secondary genre and its role, if any:
Vocals or instrumental:
Vocal character and delivery:
```

### C — Form and emotional arc

```text
Approximate duration:
Section order, or ask for a suggestion:
How the emotion should change:
Where the climax should occur:
```

### D — Harmony, instruments and sound

```text
BPM, meter and key/mode, or ask for suggestions:
Lead instrument:
Supporting instruments:
Space / production character:
Elements to avoid:
```

### E — Existing work and scope

```text
Existing lyrics or Styles:
What must stay unchanged:
What may be rewritten:
Which outputs I need this time:
Styles character budget, if any:
```

Leave out irrelevant sections. A one-sentence request is also valid.
