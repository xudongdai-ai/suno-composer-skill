## Example 1 — City pop with neo-soul color

**Request:** An English song about driving home at night, a warm female voice, approximately three minutes and a memorable chorus.

### Direction

Title: **Let Tonight Belong to Me**. The narrator stops waiting for an old relationship's reply and begins looking after herself. City pop leads the groove and song form; neo-soul contributes warm Rhodes extensions and relaxed phrasing. Suggested parameters: 108 BPM, 4/4, F major.

The vocal is the foreground. Rhodes supplies harmony, clean guitar plays syncopated accents, melodic bass connects roots and live drums maintain the backbeat. The hook is “Let tonight belong to me.”

## Example 2 — A ninety-second piano/cello instrumental

**Request:** A winter short film, restraint to warmth and back to quiet, A–B–A′, no drums or vocals.

### Direction and plan

Title: **Daylight by the Window**. Post-classical leads; ambient influence is limited to sparse depth. Suggest D minor, 80 BPM and 4/4. Piano proposes D–F–E; cello answers. B rises in register and A′ retrieves the motif.

| Section | Function | Approx. bars | Candidate chords | Instrument event | Energy |

| Intro | Establish breath | 2 | Dm(add9) | Sparse piano notes | 1 |
| A | Present the theme | 8 | Dm(add9)–Bbmaj7–F–C | Cello answers in a middle register | 2 |
| B | Allow warmer light | 8 | Fmaj7–C/E–Bbmaj7–A7 | Higher cello register; background opens slightly | 3 |
| A′ | Quiet return | 8 | Dm(add9)–Bbmaj7–Dm/A–Dm | Fewer notes; background recedes | 2 |
| Coda | Natural resonance | 4 | Dm(add9) | Cello finishes before the piano tail | 1 |

Thirty bars at 80 BPM in 4/4 plan to ninety seconds. A7 introduces C# as a minor-key leading tone; it is not diatonic to D natural minor. Exact motif notes and duration require audio verification or manual production.
