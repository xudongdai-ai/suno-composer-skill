# Sound and Mixing

**Use this category to describe tone, depth, width, attacks and the dynamic arc.** These are listening goals, not guaranteed measurements.

## Six distinct dimensions

| Dimension | Example language |
|---|---|
| Timbre | warm analog, brittle metallic, dark and woolly, transparent acoustic |
| Depth | upfront dry lead, rear hall reflections, distant atmosphere |
| Width | mono-compatible bass, stereo pads, centered voice, spread doubles |
| Transient | punchy live snare, soft brushed drums, swelled reverse attacks |
| Harmonic coloration | tube saturation, tape compression, clean digital, distorted edge |
| Large-scale dynamics | contained beginning to expansive climax; avoid making every passage equally dense |

## Choose the two most useful production priorities

| Genre | Production priorities | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Neo-soul | laid-back pocket, analog warmth, intimate vocal | drum quantization too rigid |
| City pop | polished bass/guitar separation, airy backing harmony | mushy low mids |
| Shoegaze | smeared guitar wall, vocal behind guitars, noise tails | indistinct bass line |
| Post-rock | gradual crescendo, evolving delay, deliberate space | immediate maximum density |
| Techno | tight sub/kick, restrained harmonic movement | overcrowded stereo lows |
| Liquid DnB | crisp breaks, melodic pads, controlled sub | overbearing risers |
| Baroque chamber | clear imitative lines, modest room resonance | cinematic hall drowning counterpoint |
| Cinematic score | high dynamic range, room depth, theme clarity | every bar is a climax |
| Chinese chamber | close plucked transient, breath and ornament detail | stereotypical bell overload |
| Ambient | long tails, slowly changing spectral energy | intrusive conventional drums |

## Production language that stays coherent

- `intimate dry vocal, soft tape saturation, open stereo backing harmonies`
- `tight mono sub, bright shuffled hats, short plate reverb on lead stabs`
- `natural chamber room, clear bow attacks, transparent counterpoint`
- `wide cinematic strings, deep but controlled low percussion, restrained hall reflections`

Avoid an undefined combination of fully dry intimacy and a huge hall on the same foreground source. They can be assigned to different layers or sections.

## Listening questions

Does bass compete with kick? Can the main melody be heard through the middle register? Does the chorus gain arrangement contrast or only volume? Are transitions musical rather than generic risers? Does the actual stem export provide the separation needed for the proposed edit?
