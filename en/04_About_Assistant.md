# Songwriting Assistant

A guide for GPT covering lyrics, genres, form, harmony, arrangement, instrumental music and focused revisions. Includes 81 genre profiles, 17 forms and 26 harmony prototypes.

Upload [the complete MD](01_Upload_to_GPT_Songwriting_Assistant.md), then describe your theme, intended sound and vocal or instrumental choice. Copy the relevant output fields into Suno, generate the music and listen to the result.
