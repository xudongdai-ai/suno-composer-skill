## 01. Contemporary R&B (`soul-rnb`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 60–105 BPM; 4/4 or 6/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: ii9–V13–Imaj9, modal interchange, slash voicings

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Pre–Hook–Verse–Bridge–Hook

- **Groove**: laid-back syncopation, sparse 808 or organic kit.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: intimate melismatic vocal, Rhodes.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: sub bass, guitar fills, harmonized backing voices.

- **Production and space**: detailed vocal space, silky midrange.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid heavily distorted electric guitar wall.

## 02. Neo-soul (`soul-neosoul`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 65–100 BPM; 4/4, laidback swung.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: m9–13sus–maj9, chromatic inner voice motion

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Chorus–Instrumental–Bridge–Outro

- **Groove**: behind-the-beat drums, ghost notes.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: Rhodes, expressive lead vocal or guitar.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: round bass, soft organ, backing harmony.

- **Production and space**: organic pocket, warm analog saturation.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid stiff EDM quantization.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.


## 03. Gospel soul (`soul-gospel`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 70–120 BPM; 4/4, 6/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: I–IV–V with passing diminished, secondary dominants

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Call-Response–Key-Lift–Final Refrain

- **Groove**: live backbeat with church-style push/pull.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: piano/organ, choir call-response.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: bass guitar, drums, handclaps.

- **Production and space**: roomy communal energy and big dynamic lift.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid detached robotic vocals.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Gospel soul, 70–120 BPM target, 4/4, 6/8 feel, live backbeat with church-style push/pull; lead: piano/organ, choir call-response; supporting layers: bass guitar, drums, handclaps; harmonic color: I–IV–V with passing diminished, secondary dominants; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Call-Response to Key-Lift to Final Refrain; production: roomy communal energy and big dynamic lift.
```

## 04. Funk (`soul-funk`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 90–125 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: I7 or i7 vamp, #9 blues vocabulary, dominant riffs

- **Form**: Riff–Verse–Hook–Break–Solo–Hook

- **Groove**: syncopated sixteenth-note pocket, strong one.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: slap bass, rhythm guitar chanks, horn riffs.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: clavinet, tight drums, brass stabs.

- **Production and space**: dry punchy rhythm section with close mics.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid long legato orchestral swells.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Funk, 90–125 BPM target, 4/4 feel, syncopated sixteenth-note pocket, strong one; lead: slap bass, rhythm guitar chanks, horn riffs; supporting layers: clavinet, tight drums, brass stabs; harmonic color: I7 or i7 vamp, #9 blues vocabulary, dominant riffs; structure evolving from Riff to Verse to Hook to Break to Solo to Hook; production: dry punchy rhythm section with close mics.
```

## 05. Disco (`soul-disco`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 110–130 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: maj7/6, ii–V flourishes, minor-to-major lifts

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Instrumental Break–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: four-on-floor, open hats, syncopated bass.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: string riffs, rhythmic guitar, melodic vocals.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: electric piano, horns, live percussion.

- **Production and space**: lush dancefloor orchestration, clear pulse.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid industrial distorted sub bass.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Disco, 110–130 BPM target, 4/4 feel, four-on-floor, open hats, syncopated bass; lead: string riffs, rhythmic guitar, melodic vocals; supporting layers: electric piano, horns, live percussion; harmonic color: maj7/6, ii–V flourishes, minor-to-major lifts; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Instrumental Break to Final Chorus; production: lush dancefloor orchestration, clear pulse.
```
