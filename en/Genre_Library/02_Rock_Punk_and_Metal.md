## 01. Classic rock (`rock-classic`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 90–145 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: I–bVII–IV; blues I–IV–V and pentatonic riff cells

- **Form**: Riff Intro–Verse–Chorus–Solo–Verse–Chorus–Outro

- **Groove**: live backbeat, eighth-note driving hi-hat.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: crunch electric guitar riff, expressive lead vocal.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: electric bass and acoustic drum kit.

- **Production and space**: amp-driven midrange, natural drum ambience.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid glossy quantized EDM kick.


## 02. Alternative rock (`rock-alt`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 90–145 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: power-chord pedal tone, modal mixture, bVI–bVII–i

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Chorus–Verse–Chorus–Bridge–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: dynamics from muted verse to open chorus.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: contrasting clean/distorted guitar, raw vocal.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: active bass and roomy drum kit.

- **Production and space**: punchy guitars with dynamic headroom.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid over-sweet string pads.

## 04. Shoegaze (`rock-shoegaze`)

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: pedal harmony, maj7/add9 clusters, slow modal movement

- **Form**: Guitar Bloom–Verse–Instrumental Expansion–Climax–Dissolve

- **Groove**: hypnotic drum drive under sustained wash.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: fuzz bass, delay feedback, cymbal swells.

- **Production and space**: huge enveloping stereo texture, vocal integrated into wall.

## 06. Math rock (`rock-math`)

- **Groove**: interlocking polyrhythmic accents and displaced downbeats.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: clean tapped guitar, intricate drum fills.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: melodic bass, restrained ambience.

- **Production and space**: precise transients, separated interlocking lines.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid rigid dance four-on-floor.

## 07. Progressive rock (`rock-prog`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 80–170 BPM; mixed 4/4, 5/8, 7/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: modal sequence, extended harmony, thematic reharmonization

- **Form**: Overture–Theme A–Development–Solo–Recapitulation–Coda

- **Groove**: shifting meter and long-range motivic pulse.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: guitar/keyboard thematic exchanges.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: bass countermelody, acoustic drums, synth textures.

- **Production and space**: layered dynamic mix with clearly exposed motifs.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid endless virtuosic fills without thematic logic.

## 08. Post-rock (`rock-post`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 60–130 BPM; 4/4, 6/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: two-chord/modal pedal, slowly expanding voicings

- **Form**: Minimal Intro–Ostinato–Build 1–Build 2–Crescendo–Release

- **Groove**: repeated motor figure with gradual subdivision.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: delay guitar ostinato, bowed guitar or strings.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: bass pedal, tom patterns, ambient pads.

- **Production and space**: large evolving crescendos, dynamic space.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid verse/chorus commercial vocals.

- **Groove**: double-kick patterns, syncopated chugs.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: low-tuned rhythm guitars, forceful vocal or instrumental lead.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: bass unison, tightly controlled drums.

- **Production and space**: tight low end, aggressive guitars, clear transient attack.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid soft acoustic ballad feel.

## 10. Metalcore (`rock-metalcore`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 125–180 BPM; 4/4, half-time breakdown.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: minor/Phrygian riff, melodic chorus changes

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Breakdown–Bridge–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: fast kick figures and half-time hits.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: down-tuned guitar, harsh/clean vocal contrasts.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: punch bass, atmospheric guitars.

- **Production and space**: modern tightly gated guitar, massive chorus lift.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid bright disco bounce.
