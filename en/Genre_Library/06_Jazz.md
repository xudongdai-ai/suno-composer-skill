## 01. Swing jazz (`jazz-swing`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 110–200 BPM; 4/4 swing.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: ii7–V7–Imaj7, rhythm changes, turnaround

- **Form**: Head–Improvisation–Trading–Head–Tag

- **Groove**: swung eighths and walking bass.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: trumpet/sax head melody, piano comping.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: upright bass, ride cymbal, horns.

- **Production and space**: live ensemble depth, natural transients.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid programmed rigid sixteenth hats.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.


## 02. Bebop (`jazz-bebop`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 160–260 BPM; 4/4 swing.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: chromatic approach ii–V–I, tritone substitutions, altered dominants

- **Form**: Fast Head–Improvised Choruses–Head

- **Groove**: fast ride pattern with crisp comping.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: agile sax or trumpet lines.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: piano, walking upright bass, drum kit.

- **Production and space**: clear acoustic small-combo recording.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid sustained cinematic pads.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

## 03. Cool jazz (`jazz-cool`)

- **Form**: Head–Solo–Interlude–Head

- **Groove**: restrained swing, airy pulse.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: muted trumpet, tenor sax, piano.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: brushed kit, upright bass.

- **Production and space**: intimate club space and natural dynamics.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid overdriven distorted guitars.

- **Groove**: open elastic pulse with ride cymbal.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: tenor sax, spacious piano voicings.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: bass ostinato, responsive drums.

- **Production and space**: improvisatory ensemble interaction.
