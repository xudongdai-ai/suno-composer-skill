## 01. Dark ambient (`ambient-dark`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 35–85 BPM; free, slow pulse.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: drones, minor seconds, spectral clusters

- **Form**: Drone Entrance–Texture Mutation–Shadow Peak–Fade

- **Groove**: barely perceptible, irregular impacts.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: granular low drones, bowed metal.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: noise textures, distant piano resonances.

- **Production and space**: immersive low-frequency space, slow spectral movement.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid pop backbeat and singable verse.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Dark ambient, 35–85 BPM target, free, slow pulse feel, barely perceptible, irregular impacts; lead: granular low drones, bowed metal; supporting layers: noise textures, distant piano resonances; harmonic color: drones, minor seconds, spectral clusters; structure evolving from Drone Entrance to Texture Mutation to Shadow Peak to Fade; production: immersive low-frequency space, slow spectral movement.
```

## 02. Ethereal ambient (`ambient-ethereal`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 40–85 BPM; free or 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: suspended quartal voicings, add9 and pedal

- **Form**: Sparse Opening–Gradual Layers–Airy Peak–Dissolve

- **Groove**: no drums or very soft pulse.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: soft pads, glass harmonics, breathy textures.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: field ambience, warm sub drone.

- **Production and space**: long decay, generous quiet and smooth frequency balance.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid percussion-heavy drops.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Ethereal ambient, 40–85 BPM target, free or 4/4 feel, no drums or very soft pulse; lead: soft pads, glass harmonics, breathy textures; supporting layers: field ambience, warm sub drone; harmonic color: suspended quartal voicings, add9 and pedal; structure evolving from Sparse Opening to Gradual Layers to Airy Peak to Dissolve; production: long decay, generous quiet and smooth frequency balance.
```

## 03. Drone music (`ambient-drone`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 20–70 BPM; nonmetric.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: microtonal beating, overtone-based tuning, static pedal

- **Form**: Drone–Harmonic Shift–Spectral Bloom–Silence

- **Groove**: nonmetric gradual timbral motion.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: sustained strings, organ or oscillator tones.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: granular noise, resonant bells.

- **Production and space**: wide continuous evolving textures.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid chord changes every four bars.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

Drone music, 20–70 BPM target, nonmetric feel, nonmetric gradual timbral motion; lead: sustained strings, organ or oscillator tones; supporting layers: granular noise, resonant bells; harmonic color: microtonal beating, overtone-based tuning, static pedal; structure evolving from Drone to Harmonic Shift to Spectral Bloom to Silence; production: wide continuous evolving textures.

## 04. Glitch experimental (`ambient-glitch`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 60–150 BPM; broken or polymetric.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: fragmentary tone clusters, short sampled pitch cells

- **Form**: Sparse Particles–Density Build–Breakdown–New Pattern

- **Groove**: micro-timed clicks, irregular cuts.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: granular sound design, found audio fragments.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: sub clicks, spectral pads.

- **Production and space**: intentionally disrupted precision and extreme detail.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid conventional verse-chorus formula.

## 05. Trip-hop (`ambient-triphop`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 65–100 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: minor 7th loop, chromatic bass and blues color

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Instrumental Hook–Verse–Break–Outro

- **Groove**: slow dusty breakbeat, heavy backbeat.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: smoky vocal, Rhodes, sampled strings.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: sub bass, vinyl textures, guitar fragments.

- **Production and space**: shadowy cinematic groove and dark reverb.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid shiny upbeat stadium pop.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

## 06. Post-classical (`ambient-postclassical`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 50–110 BPM; 4/4, 3/4, 6/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: simple diatonic figure with extended tertian color

- **Form**: Piano Motif–String Entry–Variation–Quiet Reprise

- **Groove**: repetitive piano pulse, expressive rubato.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: felt piano, intimate cello or violin.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: soft electronics, chamber strings.

- **Production and space**: close-miked textures with natural room air.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid epic brass impacts.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

## 07. Industrial experimental (`ambient-industrial`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 80–150 BPM; 4/4, mechanical odd cycles.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: tritone drones, distorted modal riffs

- **Form**: Mechanical Intro–Process Build–Impact–Breakdown–End

- **Groove**: metallic hits and machine-like subdivisions.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: distorted percussion, processed voice or synth.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: noise wall, low-frequency pulse.

- **Production and space**: abrasive grit, aggressive transient and distortion.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid glossy acoustic folk mood.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

Industrial experimental, 80–150 BPM target, 4/4, mechanical odd cycles feel, metallic hits and machine-like subdivisions; lead: distorted percussion, processed voice or synth; supporting layers: noise wall, low-frequency pulse; harmonic color: tritone drones, distorted modal riffs; structure evolving from Mechanical Intro to Process Build to Impact to Breakdown to End; production: abrasive grit, aggressive transient and distortion.
