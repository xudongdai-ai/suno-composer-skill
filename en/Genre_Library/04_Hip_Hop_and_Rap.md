## 01. Boom bap (`hiphop-boombap`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 82–100 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: minor jazz sample loop, ii–V fragments

- **Form**: Sample Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro

- **Groove**: swinging kick-snare with dusty hats.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: spoken-rap delivery, chopped jazz/piano loop.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: round electric bass, scratches.

- **Production and space**: tape grit and compact mono-ish core.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid bright stadium EDM build.

## 02. Trap (`hiphop-trap`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 120–160 (half-time feel) BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: dark minor 2–4 chord loop, Phrygian color

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro

- **Groove**: 808 kick, rapid triplet hats and sparse snare.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: 808 bass slides, dark bell motif, rap lead.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: airy pad, percussion ear candy.

- **Production and space**: bass-weighted tight modern mix.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid live jazz swing drum kit as main groove.

## 03. Drill (`hiphop-drill`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 130–150 BPM; 4/4 half-time.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: i–bVI–bVII/minor ostinato

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro

- **Groove**: sliding 808 with syncopated hats and snare.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: dissonant piano or strings, clipped vocal phrasing.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: low brass stabs, sparse FX.

- **Production and space**: cold tense minimal low-end production.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid upbeat disco chord sparkle.

## 04. Lo-fi hip-hop (`hiphop-lofi`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 65–95 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: maj7–m7–9th loops, ii–V fragments

- **Form**: Texture Intro–Loop A–Variation B–Loop A′–Fade

- **Groove**: soft swung kick/snare, understated pocket.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: Rhodes or mellow jazz guitar motif.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: upright/electric bass, noise floor.

- **Production and space**: dusty tape warmth, softened transients.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid festival risers and loud drops.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.
