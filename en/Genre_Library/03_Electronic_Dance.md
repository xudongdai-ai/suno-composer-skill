## 01. House (`edm-house`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 118–130 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: short minor/major triad loops, 7ths optional

- **Form**: DJ Intro–Groove–Break–Build–Drop–Break–Drop–DJ Outro

- **Groove**: four-on-the-floor, offbeat hats, sidechained bass.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: organ or piano stab, repeating vocal chop.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: sub bass, percussion shakers, filtered pad.

- **Production and space**: club-forward kick-bass coupling and clean top.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid complex free-rubato phrases.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
House, 118–130 BPM target, 4/4 feel, four-on-the-floor, offbeat hats, sidechained bass; lead: organ or piano stab, repeating vocal chop; supporting layers: sub bass, percussion shakers, filtered pad; harmonic color: short minor/major triad loops, 7ths optional; structure evolving from DJ Intro to Groove to Break to Build to Drop to Break to Drop to DJ Outro; production: club-forward kick-bass coupling and clean top.
```
