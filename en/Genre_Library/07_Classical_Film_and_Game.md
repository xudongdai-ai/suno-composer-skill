## 01. Baroque counterpoint (`orchestral-baroque`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 65–135 BPM; 2/4, 3/4, 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: functional sequences, circle of fifths, suspensions 4–3/7–6

- **Form**: Exposition–Episode–Middle Entries–Stretto/Final Entry

- **Groove**: motoric contrapuntal eighth/sixteenth movement.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: subject in violin/harpsichord/oboe.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: basso continuo and imitative countervoices.

- **Production and space**: clear chamber texture, minimal romantic reverb.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid modern pop drum kit.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.
