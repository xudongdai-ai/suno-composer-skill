# Pop and Songwriting

Part of the 81-profile library. Choose one primary genre, then add a secondary influence only when it has a clear role.

These are adaptable writing prototypes. Regional, historical and stylistic variants may differ. Apply harmony to the actual mode and melody. Create audible section contrast through changing instrumentation, inversions, counterpoint or drum intensity. Original Styles examples describe targets; they do not lock chords, duration or structure.

## 01. Contemporary pop (`pop-mainstream`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 95–128 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: I–V–vi–IV or vi–IV–I–V; four-chord loop with pre-chorus dominant tension

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Bridge–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: tight backbeat, eighth-note bass and syncopated accents.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: focused vocal hook, piano or synth motif.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: sub bass, bright keys, layered backing vocals.

- **Production and space**: radio-forward vocal, clean low end, wide but uncluttered hook.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid complex jazz substitutions that obscure the hook.

## 02. Synth-pop (`pop-synth`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 100–125 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: vi–IV–I–V; add9 and suspended colors

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Chorus–Verse–Chorus–Middle Eight–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: steady electronic kick, pulsing eighth-note arpeggio.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: analog lead synth, memorable vocal.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: poly pads, sequenced bass, gated snare.

- **Production and space**: retro-leaning synth sheen, crisp transient control.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid acoustic folk strumming as main identity.

## 03. Dream pop (`pop-dream`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 75–115 BPM; 4/4 or 6/8.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: maj7–add9 loops, ambiguous tonic, slow harmonic rhythm

- **Form**: Texture Intro–Verse–Refrain–Instrumental–Refrain–Outro

- **Groove**: soft and floating, understated snare.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: breathy vocal, chorused guitar line.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: shimmering pads, soft bass, washed guitar.

- **Production and space**: diffuse reverberant layers, warm high end.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid dry punchy hard-rock drums.

## 04. Art pop (`pop-art`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 80–130 BPM; 4/4 with occasional odd-bar phrase.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: borrowed chords, chromatic mediants, modal mixture, deceptive cadences

- **Form**: A–B–C–A′ with a contrasting textural pivot

- **Groove**: asymmetric accents or restrained electronic pulse.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: characterful vocal, prepared piano or unusual synth.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: chamber strings, found percussion, counter-lines.

- **Production and space**: experimental yet intelligible foreground.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid generic four-on-floor drop unless specified.

**Original Suno Styles example** — shorten to fit your current field budget; adapt to the actual work.

```text
Art pop, 80–130 BPM target, 4/4 with occasional odd-bar phrase feel, asymmetric accents or restrained electronic pulse; lead: characterful vocal, prepared piano or unusual synth; supporting layers: chamber strings, found percussion, counter-lines; harmonic color: borrowed chords, chromatic mediants, modal mixture, deceptive cadences; structure evolving from A to B to C to A′ with a contrasting textural pivot; production: experimental yet intelligible foreground.
```

## 09. J-pop (`pop-j`)

- **Tempo / Metre**: 115–160 BPM; 4/4.

- **Harmony and tonal vocabulary**: secondary dominants, ii–V motion, borrowed iv, bright modulations

- **Form**: Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Chorus–Instrumental–Bridge–Final Chorus

- **Groove**: driving double-time energy with syncopated chords.

- **Lead instruments and solo roles**: high-register vocal melody, piano or guitar hook.

- **Supporting instruments and rhythm layers**: active bass, layered synth, tight drums.

- **Production and space**: dense energetic arrangement with readable sections.

- **Avoid and boundaries**: avoid excessive pad wash that hides the melody.
