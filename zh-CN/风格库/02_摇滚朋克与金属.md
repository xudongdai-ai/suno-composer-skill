# 摇滚、朋克与金属

属于 81 风格库，按需要选一个主风格，再决定是否融合。

**使用说明**：每个条目是专业创作原型；不同地区、年代、流派内部变体可以偏离这些数据。Style Prompt 均为原创示例，不保证模型严格遵循。

## 01. 经典摇滚 / Classic rock (`rock-classic`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：90–145 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I–bVII–IV; blues I–IV–V and pentatonic riff cells。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Riff Intro–Verse–Chorus–Solo–Verse–Chorus–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：live backbeat, eighth-note driving hi-hat。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：crunch electric guitar riff, expressive lead vocal。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：electric bass and acoustic drum kit。
- **制作与声音空间**：amp-driven midrange, natural drum ambience。
- **排除和边界**：avoid glossy quantized EDM kick。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Classic rock, 90–145 BPM target, 4/4 feel, live backbeat, eighth-note driving hi-hat; lead: crunch electric guitar riff, expressive lead vocal; supporting layers: electric bass and acoustic drum kit; harmonic color: I–bVII–IV; blues I–IV–V and pentatonic riff cells; structure evolving from Riff Intro to Verse to Chorus to Solo to Verse to Chorus to Outro; production: amp-driven midrange, natural drum ambience.
```

- **背景配器与节奏层**：active bass and roomy drum kit。
- **制作与声音空间**：punchy guitars with dynamic headroom。
- **排除和边界**：avoid over-sweet string pads。

## 06. 数学摇滚 / Math rock (`rock-math`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：90–160 BPM；5/4, 7/8, alternating meters。
- **和声与调性语汇**：add9, suspended voicings, tapped dyads, cyclical motifs。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Motif A–Developed A–Contrasting B–Return A′。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：interlocking polyrhythmic accents and displaced downbeats。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：clean tapped guitar, intricate drum fills。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：melodic bass, restrained ambience。
- **制作与声音空间**：precise transients, separated interlocking lines。
- **排除和边界**：avoid rigid dance four-on-floor。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Math rock, 90–160 BPM target, 5/4, 7/8, alternating meters feel, interlocking polyrhythmic accents and displaced downbeats; lead: clean tapped guitar, intricate drum fills; supporting layers: melodic bass, restrained ambience; harmonic color: add9, suspended voicings, tapped dyads, cyclical motifs; structure evolving from Motif A to Developed A to Contrasting B to Return A′; production: precise transients, separated interlocking lines.
```

## 07. 前卫摇滚 / Progressive rock (`rock-prog`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：80–170 BPM；mixed 4/4, 5/8, 7/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：modal sequence, extended harmony, thematic reharmonization。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Overture–Theme A–Development–Solo–Recapitulation–Coda。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：shifting meter and long-range motivic pulse。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：guitar/keyboard thematic exchanges。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：bass countermelody, acoustic drums, synth textures。
- **制作与声音空间**：layered dynamic mix with clearly exposed motifs。
- **排除和边界**：avoid endless virtuosic fills without thematic logic。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Progressive rock, 80–170 BPM target, mixed 4/4, 5/8, 7/8 feel, shifting meter and long-range motivic pulse; lead: guitar/keyboard thematic exchanges; supporting layers: bass countermelody, acoustic drums, synth textures; harmonic color: modal sequence, extended harmony, thematic reharmonization; structure evolving from Overture to Theme A to Development to Solo to Recapitulation to Coda; production: layered dynamic mix with clearly exposed motifs.
```

## 08. 后摇滚 / Post-rock (`rock-post`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：60–130 BPM；4/4, 6/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：two-chord/modal pedal, slowly expanding voicings。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Minimal Intro–Ostinato–Build 1–Build 2–Crescendo–Release。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：repeated motor figure with gradual subdivision。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：delay guitar ostinato, bowed guitar or strings。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：bass pedal, tom patterns, ambient pads。
- **制作与声音空间**：large evolving crescendos, dynamic space。
- **排除和边界**：avoid verse/chorus commercial vocals。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Post-rock, 60–130 BPM target, 4/4, 6/8 feel, repeated motor figure with gradual subdivision; lead: delay guitar ostinato, bowed guitar or strings; supporting layers: bass pedal, tom patterns, ambient pads; harmonic color: two-chord/modal pedal, slowly expanding voicings; structure evolving from Minimal Intro to Ostinato to Build 1 to Build 2 to Crescendo to Release; production: large evolving crescendos, dynamic space.
```

- **律动 / Groove**：fast kick figures and half-time hits。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：down-tuned guitar, harsh/clean vocal contrasts。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：punch bass, atmospheric guitars。
- **制作与声音空间**：modern tightly gated guitar, massive chorus lift。
- **排除和边界**：avoid bright disco bounce。
