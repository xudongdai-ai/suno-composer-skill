# 嘻哈与说唱

属于 81 风格库，按需要选一个主风格，再决定是否融合。

**使用说明**：每个条目是专业创作原型；不同地区、年代、流派内部变体可以偏离这些数据。Style Prompt 均为原创示例，不保证模型严格遵循。

## 01. 经典嘻哈 / Boom bap (`hiphop-boombap`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：82–100 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：minor jazz sample loop, ii–V fragments。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Sample Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：swinging kick-snare with dusty hats。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：spoken-rap delivery, chopped jazz/piano loop。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：round electric bass, scratches。
- **制作与声音空间**：tape grit and compact mono-ish core。
- **排除和边界**：avoid bright stadium EDM build。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Boom bap, 82–100 BPM target, 4/4 feel, swinging kick-snare with dusty hats; lead: spoken-rap delivery, chopped jazz/piano loop; supporting layers: round electric bass, scratches; harmonic color: minor jazz sample loop, ii–V fragments; structure evolving from Sample Intro to Verse to Hook to Verse to Hook to Outro; production: tape grit and compact mono-ish core.
```

## 02. Trap / Trap (`hiphop-trap`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：120–160 (half-time feel) BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：dark minor 2–4 chord loop, Phrygian color。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：808 kick, rapid triplet hats and sparse snare。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：808 bass slides, dark bell motif, rap lead。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：airy pad, percussion ear candy。
- **制作与声音空间**：bass-weighted tight modern mix。
- **排除和边界**：avoid live jazz swing drum kit as main groove。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Trap, 120–160 (half-time feel) BPM target, 4/4 feel, 808 kick, rapid triplet hats and sparse snare; lead: 808 bass slides, dark bell motif, rap lead; supporting layers: airy pad, percussion ear candy; harmonic color: dark minor 2–4 chord loop, Phrygian color; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Hook to Verse to Hook to Outro; production: bass-weighted tight modern mix.
```

## 03. Drill / Drill (`hiphop-drill`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：130–150 BPM；4/4 half-time。
- **和声与调性语汇**：i–bVI–bVII/minor ostinato。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Hook–Verse–Hook–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：sliding 808 with syncopated hats and snare。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：dissonant piano or strings, clipped vocal phrasing。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：low brass stabs, sparse FX。
- **制作与声音空间**：cold tense minimal low-end production。
- **排除和边界**：avoid upbeat disco chord sparkle。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Drill, 130–150 BPM target, 4/4 half-time feel, sliding 808 with syncopated hats and snare; lead: dissonant piano or strings, clipped vocal phrasing; supporting layers: low brass stabs, sparse FX; harmonic color: i–bVI–bVII/minor ostinato; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Hook to Verse to Hook to Outro; production: cold tense minimal low-end production.
```

## 04. 低保真嘻哈 / Lo-fi hip-hop (`hiphop-lofi`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：65–95 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：maj7–m7–9th loops, ii–V fragments。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Texture Intro–Loop A–Variation B–Loop A′–Fade。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：soft swung kick/snare, understated pocket。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：Rhodes or mellow jazz guitar motif。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：upright/electric bass, noise floor。
- **制作与声音空间**：dusty tape warmth, softened transients。
- **排除和边界**：avoid festival risers and loud drops。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Lo-fi hip-hop, 65–95 BPM target, 4/4 feel, soft swung kick/snare, understated pocket; lead: Rhodes or mellow jazz guitar motif; supporting layers: upright/electric bass, noise floor; harmonic color: maj7–m7–9th loops, ii–V fragments; structure evolving from Texture Intro to Loop A to Variation B to Loop A′ to Fade; production: dusty tape warmth, softened transients.
```
