# 灵魂乐、R&B 与放克

属于 81 风格库，按需要选一个主风格，再决定是否融合。

**使用说明**：每个条目是专业创作原型；不同地区、年代、流派内部变体可以偏离这些数据。Style Prompt 均为原创示例，不保证模型严格遵循。

## 01. 当代节奏布鲁斯 / Contemporary R&B (`soul-rnb`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：60–105 BPM；4/4 or 6/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：ii9–V13–Imaj9, modal interchange, slash voicings。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Hook–Verse–Bridge–Hook。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：laid-back syncopation, sparse 808 or organic kit。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：intimate melismatic vocal, Rhodes。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：sub bass, guitar fills, harmonized backing voices。
- **制作与声音空间**：detailed vocal space, silky midrange。
- **排除和边界**：avoid heavily distorted electric guitar wall。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Contemporary R&B, 60–105 BPM target, 4/4 or 6/8 feel, laid-back syncopation, sparse 808 or organic kit; lead: intimate melismatic vocal, Rhodes; supporting layers: sub bass, guitar fills, harmonized backing voices; harmonic color: ii9–V13–Imaj9, modal interchange, slash voicings; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Hook to Verse to Bridge to Hook; production: detailed vocal space, silky midrange.
```

## 02. 新灵魂乐 / Neo-soul (`soul-neosoul`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：65–100 BPM；4/4, laidback swung。
- **和声与调性语汇**：m9–13sus–maj9, chromatic inner voice motion。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Chorus–Instrumental–Bridge–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：behind-the-beat drums, ghost notes。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：Rhodes, expressive lead vocal or guitar。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：round bass, soft organ, backing harmony。
- **制作与声音空间**：organic pocket, warm analog saturation。
- **排除和边界**：avoid stiff EDM quantization。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Neo-soul, 65–100 BPM target, 4/4, laidback swung feel, behind-the-beat drums, ghost notes; lead: Rhodes, expressive lead vocal or guitar; supporting layers: round bass, soft organ, backing harmony; harmonic color: m9–13sus–maj9, chromatic inner voice motion; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Chorus to Instrumental to Bridge to Outro; production: organic pocket, warm analog saturation.
```

## 03. 福音灵魂 / Gospel soul (`soul-gospel`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：70–120 BPM；4/4, 6/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I–IV–V with passing diminished, secondary dominants。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Call-Response–Key-Lift–Final Refrain。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：live backbeat with church-style push/pull。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：piano/organ, choir call-response。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：bass guitar, drums, handclaps。
- **制作与声音空间**：roomy communal energy and big dynamic lift。
- **排除和边界**：avoid detached robotic vocals。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Gospel soul, 70–120 BPM target, 4/4, 6/8 feel, live backbeat with church-style push/pull; lead: piano/organ, choir call-response; supporting layers: bass guitar, drums, handclaps; harmonic color: I–IV–V with passing diminished, secondary dominants; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Call-Response to Key-Lift to Final Refrain; production: roomy communal energy and big dynamic lift.
```

## 04. 放克 / Funk (`soul-funk`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：90–125 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I7 or i7 vamp, #9 blues vocabulary, dominant riffs。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Riff–Verse–Hook–Break–Solo–Hook。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：syncopated sixteenth-note pocket, strong one。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：slap bass, rhythm guitar chanks, horn riffs。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：clavinet, tight drums, brass stabs。
- **制作与声音空间**：dry punchy rhythm section with close mics。
- **排除和边界**：avoid long legato orchestral swells。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Funk, 90–125 BPM target, 4/4 feel, syncopated sixteenth-note pocket, strong one; lead: slap bass, rhythm guitar chanks, horn riffs; supporting layers: clavinet, tight drums, brass stabs; harmonic color: I7 or i7 vamp, #9 blues vocabulary, dominant riffs; structure evolving from Riff to Verse to Hook to Break to Solo to Hook; production: dry punchy rhythm section with close mics.
```

## 05. 迪斯科 / Disco (`soul-disco`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：110–130 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：maj7/6, ii–V flourishes, minor-to-major lifts。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Instrumental Break–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：four-on-floor, open hats, syncopated bass。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：string riffs, rhythmic guitar, melodic vocals。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：electric piano, horns, live percussion。
- **制作与声音空间**：lush dancefloor orchestration, clear pulse。
- **排除和边界**：avoid industrial distorted sub bass。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Disco, 110–130 BPM target, 4/4 feel, four-on-floor, open hats, syncopated bass; lead: string riffs, rhythmic guitar, melodic vocals; supporting layers: electric piano, horns, live percussion; harmonic color: maj7/6, ii–V flourishes, minor-to-major lifts; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Instrumental Break to Final Chorus; production: lush dancefloor orchestration, clear pulse.
```
