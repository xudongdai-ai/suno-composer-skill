# 环境、实验与后古典

属于 81 风格库，按需要选一个主风格，再决定是否融合。

**使用说明**：每个条目是专业创作原型；不同地区、年代、流派内部变体可以偏离这些数据。Style Prompt 均为原创示例，不保证模型严格遵循。

## 01. 黑暗氛围 / Dark ambient (`ambient-dark`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：35–85 BPM；free, slow pulse。
- **和声与调性语汇**：drones, minor seconds, spectral clusters。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Drone Entrance–Texture Mutation–Shadow Peak–Fade。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：barely perceptible, irregular impacts。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：granular low drones, bowed metal。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：noise textures, distant piano resonances。
- **制作与声音空间**：immersive low-frequency space, slow spectral movement。
- **排除和边界**：avoid pop backbeat and singable verse。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Dark ambient, 35–85 BPM target, free, slow pulse feel, barely perceptible, irregular impacts; lead: granular low drones, bowed metal; supporting layers: noise textures, distant piano resonances; harmonic color: drones, minor seconds, spectral clusters; structure evolving from Drone Entrance to Texture Mutation to Shadow Peak to Fade; production: immersive low-frequency space, slow spectral movement.
```

## 02. 空灵氛围 / Ethereal ambient (`ambient-ethereal`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：40–85 BPM；free or 4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：suspended quartal voicings, add9 and pedal。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Sparse Opening–Gradual Layers–Airy Peak–Dissolve。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：no drums or very soft pulse。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：soft pads, glass harmonics, breathy textures。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：field ambience, warm sub drone。
- **制作与声音空间**：long decay, generous quiet and smooth frequency balance。
- **排除和边界**：avoid percussion-heavy drops。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Ethereal ambient, 40–85 BPM target, free or 4/4 feel, no drums or very soft pulse; lead: soft pads, glass harmonics, breathy textures; supporting layers: field ambience, warm sub drone; harmonic color: suspended quartal voicings, add9 and pedal; structure evolving from Sparse Opening to Gradual Layers to Airy Peak to Dissolve; production: long decay, generous quiet and smooth frequency balance.
```

## 03. 无人声持续音 / Drone music (`ambient-drone`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：20–70 BPM；nonmetric。
- **和声与调性语汇**：microtonal beating, overtone-based tuning, static pedal。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Drone–Harmonic Shift–Spectral Bloom–Silence。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：nonmetric gradual timbral motion。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：sustained strings, organ or oscillator tones。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：granular noise, resonant bells。
- **制作与声音空间**：wide continuous evolving textures。
- **排除和边界**：avoid chord changes every four bars。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Drone music, 20–70 BPM target, nonmetric feel, nonmetric gradual timbral motion; lead: sustained strings, organ or oscillator tones; supporting layers: granular noise, resonant bells; harmonic color: microtonal beating, overtone-based tuning, static pedal; structure evolving from Drone to Harmonic Shift to Spectral Bloom to Silence; production: wide continuous evolving textures.
```

- **背景配器与节奏层**：soft electronics, chamber strings。
