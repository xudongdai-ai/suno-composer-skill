# 流行与唱作

属于 81 风格库，按需要选一个主风格，再决定是否融合。

**使用说明**：每个条目是专业创作原型；不同地区、年代、流派内部变体可以偏离这些数据。Style Prompt 均为原创示例，不保证模型严格遵循。

## 01. 主流流行 / Contemporary pop (`pop-mainstream`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：95–128 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I–V–vi–IV or vi–IV–I–V; four-chord loop with pre-chorus dominant tension。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Bridge–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：tight backbeat, eighth-note bass and syncopated accents。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：focused vocal hook, piano or synth motif。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：sub bass, bright keys, layered backing vocals。
- **制作与声音空间**：radio-forward vocal, clean low end, wide but uncluttered hook。
- **排除和边界**：avoid complex jazz substitutions that obscure the hook。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Contemporary pop, 95–128 BPM target, 4/4 feel, tight backbeat, eighth-note bass and syncopated accents; lead: focused vocal hook, piano or synth motif; supporting layers: sub bass, bright keys, layered backing vocals; harmonic color: I–V–vi–IV or vi–IV–I–V; four-chord loop with pre-chorus dominant tension; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Bridge to Final Chorus; production: radio-forward vocal, clean low end, wide but uncluttered hook.
```

## 02. 合成器流行 / Synth-pop (`pop-synth`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：100–125 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：vi–IV–I–V; add9 and suspended colors。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Chorus–Verse–Chorus–Middle Eight–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：steady electronic kick, pulsing eighth-note arpeggio。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：analog lead synth, memorable vocal。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：poly pads, sequenced bass, gated snare。
- **制作与声音空间**：retro-leaning synth sheen, crisp transient control。
- **排除和边界**：avoid acoustic folk strumming as main identity。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Synth-pop, 100–125 BPM target, 4/4 feel, steady electronic kick, pulsing eighth-note arpeggio; lead: analog lead synth, memorable vocal; supporting layers: poly pads, sequenced bass, gated snare; harmonic color: vi–IV–I–V; add9 and suspended colors; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Chorus to Verse to Chorus to Middle Eight to Final Chorus; production: retro-leaning synth sheen, crisp transient control.
```

## 03. 梦幻流行 / Dream pop (`pop-dream`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：75–115 BPM；4/4 or 6/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：maj7–add9 loops, ambiguous tonic, slow harmonic rhythm。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Texture Intro–Verse–Refrain–Instrumental–Refrain–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：soft and floating, understated snare。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：breathy vocal, chorused guitar line。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：shimmering pads, soft bass, washed guitar。
- **制作与声音空间**：diffuse reverberant layers, warm high end。
- **排除和边界**：avoid dry punchy hard-rock drums。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Dream pop, 75–115 BPM target, 4/4 or 6/8 feel, soft and floating, understated snare; lead: breathy vocal, chorused guitar line; supporting layers: shimmering pads, soft bass, washed guitar; harmonic color: maj7–add9 loops, ambiguous tonic, slow harmonic rhythm; structure evolving from Texture Intro to Verse to Refrain to Instrumental to Refrain to Outro; production: diffuse reverberant layers, warm high end.
```

## 04. 艺术流行 / Art pop (`pop-art`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：80–130 BPM；4/4 with occasional odd-bar phrase。
- **和声与调性语汇**：borrowed chords, chromatic mediants, modal mixture, deceptive cadences。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：A–B–C–A′ with a contrasting textural pivot。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：asymmetric accents or restrained electronic pulse。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：characterful vocal, prepared piano or unusual synth。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：chamber strings, found percussion, counter-lines。
- **制作与声音空间**：experimental yet intelligible foreground。
- **排除和边界**：avoid generic four-on-floor drop unless specified。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Art pop, 80–130 BPM target, 4/4 with occasional odd-bar phrase feel, asymmetric accents or restrained electronic pulse; lead: characterful vocal, prepared piano or unusual synth; supporting layers: chamber strings, found percussion, counter-lines; harmonic color: borrowed chords, chromatic mediants, modal mixture, deceptive cadences; structure evolving from A to B to C to A′ with a contrasting textural pivot; production: experimental yet intelligible foreground.
```

## 05. 独立流行 / Indie pop (`pop-indie`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：90–130 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I–iii–IV–iv or I–V–IV–iv; bittersweet modal borrowing。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Chorus–Verse–Chorus–Outro。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：live-kit backbeat with slight humanization。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：jangly guitar, natural vocal。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：electric bass, organ, small backing choir。
- **制作与声音空间**：intimate live-room warmth, imperfect charm。
- **排除和边界**：avoid hyper-polished EDM risers。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Indie pop, 90–130 BPM target, 4/4 feel, live-kit backbeat with slight humanization; lead: jangly guitar, natural vocal; supporting layers: electric bass, organ, small backing choir; harmonic color: I–iii–IV–iv or I–V–IV–iv; bittersweet modal borrowing; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Chorus to Verse to Chorus to Outro; production: intimate live-room warmth, imperfect charm.
```

## 06. 韩流流行 / K-pop-inspired pop (`pop-k`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：95–145 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：minor-key pop loop, sus chords, dramatic pre-chorus lifts。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Post–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Bridge–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：dance pulse, half-time switch, rhythmic vocal phrases。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：stacked topline, synth stabs, rap-adjacent passage。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：sub bass, bright percussion, vocal doubles。
- **制作与声音空间**：high-definition sectional contrast and hook layering。
- **排除和边界**：avoid abrupt genre switching without a transition。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
K-pop-inspired pop, 95–145 BPM target, 4/4 feel, dance pulse, half-time switch, rhythmic vocal phrases; lead: stacked topline, synth stabs, rap-adjacent passage; supporting layers: sub bass, bright percussion, vocal doubles; harmonic color: minor-key pop loop, sus chords, dramatic pre-chorus lifts; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Post to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Bridge to Final Chorus; production: high-definition sectional contrast and hook layering.
```

## 07. 都市流行 / City pop (`pop-city`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：95–118 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：ii7–V7–Imaj7, IVmaj7–iii7–vi7; 9ths and 13ths。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Instrumental Break–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：relaxed funk syncopation, disco inflection。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：Rhodes, clean electric guitar, smooth lead vocal。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：slap or picked bass, brass accents, synth strings。
- **制作与声音空间**：glossy late-70s/80s city-night atmosphere。
- **排除和边界**：avoid maximal aggressive EDM bass。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
City pop, 95–118 BPM target, 4/4 feel, relaxed funk syncopation, disco inflection; lead: Rhodes, clean electric guitar, smooth lead vocal; supporting layers: slap or picked bass, brass accents, synth strings; harmonic color: ii7–V7–Imaj7, IVmaj7–iii7–vi7; 9ths and 13ths; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Instrumental Break to Final Chorus; production: glossy late-70s/80s city-night atmosphere.
```

## 08. 华语抒情流行 / Mandarin pop ballad (`pop-mandarin`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：62–92 BPM；4/4 or 6/8。
- **和声与调性语汇**：I–V/3–vi–IV; IV–iv–I emotional borrowing。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Piano Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Chorus–Bridge–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：light kit, rubato lead-in, backbeat grows gradually。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：clear Chinese lead vocal, melodic piano。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：acoustic guitar, lyrical strings, subtle electric bass。
- **制作与声音空间**：close lead vocal, controlled crescendo。
- **排除和边界**：avoid oversized trailer percussion。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
Mandarin pop ballad, 62–92 BPM target, 4/4 or 6/8 feel, light kit, rubato lead-in, backbeat grows gradually; lead: clear Chinese lead vocal, melodic piano; supporting layers: acoustic guitar, lyrical strings, subtle electric bass; harmonic color: I–V/3–vi–IV; IV–iv–I emotional borrowing; structure evolving from Piano Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Chorus to Bridge to Final Chorus; production: close lead vocal, controlled crescendo.
```

## 09. 日式流行 / J-pop (`pop-j`)

- **Tempo / Metre**：115–160 BPM；4/4。
- **和声与调性语汇**：secondary dominants, ii–V motion, borrowed iv, bright modulations。应按实际调式与旋律音处理，而非机械套公式。
- **曲式设计**：Intro–Verse–Pre–Chorus–Chorus–Instrumental–Bridge–Final Chorus。各段使用增减声部、转位、对位或鼓组强弱形成可听对比。
- **律动 / Groove**：driving double-time energy with syncopated chords。
- **核心乐器与独奏职责**：high-register vocal melody, piano or guitar hook。
- **背景配器与节奏层**：active bass, layered synth, tight drums。
- **制作与声音空间**：dense energetic arrangement with readable sections。
- **排除和边界**：avoid excessive pad wash that hides the melody。
- **英文 Suno Styles 范例**（可根据实际长度预算删减；不是对和弦、时长、结构的锁定）：

```text
J-pop, 115–160 BPM target, 4/4 feel, driving double-time energy with syncopated chords; lead: high-register vocal melody, piano or guitar hook; supporting layers: active bass, layered synth, tight drums; harmonic color: secondary dominants, ii–V motion, borrowed iv, bright modulations; structure evolving from Intro to Verse to Pre to Chorus to Chorus to Instrumental to Bridge to Final Chorus; production: dense energetic arrangement with readable sections.
```
